# 素材ライセンス確認の問い合わせ文面

## 宛先

chasersgaming（itch.io のアセットページのコメント欄、または itch.io のプロフィールに記載の連絡先）
- 対象: [Asset Pack 'Football,Soccer' (NES)](https://chasersgaming.itch.io/asset-pack-football-soccer)

## なぜ確認が必要か

同じページ内で作者の記述が食い違っているため。

- football パックについて: 「*this particular asset(footaball) is CC0, public domain ... with no credit necessary*」
- カタログ全体の方針として: 「*If you make modifications and you share them you must use the same license, CC-BY-SA or CC-BY-4.0, and credit me 'Chasersgaming'*」

CC0 なら制約なし、CC-BY-SA なら**改変したアート資産を同ライセンスで公開する義務**が発生し、
将来の Steam 販売の判断に影響する。**購入前に書面で確定させ、回答のスクリーンショットを保全すること。**

## 送信する文面（英語・そのまま使えます）

> Subject: License clarification for "Asset Pack 'Football,Soccer' (NES)"
>
> Hi Chasersgaming,
>
> I'm developing an original top-down soccer game and I'm considering buying your
> "Asset Pack 'Football,Soccer' (NES)". Before purchasing, I'd like to confirm the license,
> because I found two statements on the page that seem to conflict:
>
> 1. In a comment about this specific pack, you wrote that it is **CC0 / public domain**,
>    free to use commercially, modifiable, with no credit necessary.
> 2. In another comment describing your assets more generally, you wrote that modifications
>    which are shared must use **the same license (CC-BY-SA or CC-BY 4.0)** and credit you.
>
> Could you confirm which one applies to the Football/Soccer pack specifically?
>
> To be concrete, here is what I would like to do:
>
> - Modify the sprites (recolour the kits to create multiple teams, and possibly edit
>   individual frames)
> - Ship those modified sprites inside a commercial game (browser first, potentially
>   Steam later)
> - Keep my game's source code closed
>
> The key question for me is: **would I be required to publish the modified artwork under
> a share-alike licence (CC-BY-SA), or is the pack genuinely CC0 so that no such obligation
> applies?**
>
> I'm happy to credit you either way — I just need to know whether crediting is optional
> or mandatory, and whether share-alike applies.
>
> Thank you very much for your work and for taking the time.
>
> Best regards,
> （あなたのお名前 / プロジェクト名）

## 回答が来たらやること

1. 回答のスクリーンショットを保存（日付が分かる形で）
2. `docs/asset-credits.md` の表に、確定したライセンスと確認日・確認方法を記録
3. **CC-BY-SA だった場合は採用しない**（V-3 のユーザー判断: 継承義務のある素材は避ける）
   → 代替として [8-Directional Game Boy Character Template](https://gibbongl.itch.io/8-directional-gameboy-character-template)（CC0・無料・Aseprite生ファイル同梱）を素体にする
